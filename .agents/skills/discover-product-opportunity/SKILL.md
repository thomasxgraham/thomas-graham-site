# Discover Product Opportunity

Keep the work in the problem space until the customer, journey, pain, alternatives, and evidence are clear.

## Validate prerequisites

Identify the product or opportunity, target market, known evidence, constraints, and accountable decision owner. If any are absent, label them unknown and prepare the smallest evidence-gathering action.

Never invent participants, interview results, quotes, behaviors, or personas. Label hypothetical and synthetic material as design input, never research evidence.

## Run discovery

Read `references/discovery-framework.md`, then:

1. Map the customer journey end to end, including triggers, decisions, workarounds, actors, states, and outcomes.
2. Identify problems without proposing features.
3. Assess pain, impact, frequency, current alternatives, team advantage, competitor position, behavior change, and risks.
4. State a falsifiable hypothesis: target customer + problem + proposed mechanism + observable outcome.
5. Prepare non-leading interviews that begin broad, use silence, follow important threads, and repeatedly ask why without coaching.
6. Synthesize patterns and contradictions from traceable notes.
7. Define personas by characteristics, behaviors, motivations, and goals that change product use or value. Distinguish users, buyers, approvers, and affected non-users.
8. Recommend proceed, continue research, narrow, pivot, or stop.

Use `assets/discovery-packet-template.md` for durable work.

## Apply the opportunity gate

Return `PASS` only when the selected problem and hypothesis have traceable evidence. Return `FAIL` when evidence is absent, contradicted, stale, inaccessible, or too weak for commitment.

Use `WAIVER` only for bounded exploration. Name the risk owner, compensating control, evidence-acquisition plan, exposure, and expiry. Do not treat a research gap as proof.

## Include AI-specific discovery

When AI is proposed, examine whether AI is necessary, what decision it influences, who bears error, what data would be required and permitted, what human alternative exists, how uncertainty should be shown, and what uses must be prohibited. Do not select a model or implementation before the problem is established.

## Output

Lead with the selected problem or next research action. Include journey, hypothesis, evidence ledger IDs, alternatives, persona implications, contradictions, unknowns, risks, and gate status. End with one owner and next action.
