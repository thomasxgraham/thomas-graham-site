# Concept Validation Framework

## Product critique

For related and unrelated products, document:

- target user and problem;
- context and key task;
- design choices that improve usability or effectiveness;
- failure points, exclusions, and improvement opportunities;
- transferable principles, not copied surfaces.

## Prototype

Use the simplest artifact that simulates the relevant experience. Specify:

- goal of each screen or step;
- content and visuals needed for comprehension;
- user inputs;
- available actions;
- navigation and consequences;
- flexibility, scalability, accessibility, error, and recovery states;
- what is intentionally fake or omitted.

Do not spend effort on final content or engineering when lower fidelity can answer the question.

## Study design

Define target segments, recruitment/consent boundaries, tasks, scenario, moderator behavior, measures, success/failure thresholds, finding severity, analysis, and decision rule before sessions.

Preserve natural behavior. Do not coach users through the design, lead answers, or convert generated/synthetic behavior into customer evidence.

## AI concept validation

Test comprehension of AI involvement and uncertainty, intended/prohibited use, representative and edge-case tasks, data availability/rights, output quality, segment safety, human escalation, latency, cost, fallback, and failure recovery. Version the model/configuration and evaluation set.

## Cornell grounding

This framework synthesizes the CTECH104 related/unrelated critiques and prototype best-practices tool, extended with AI feasibility and safety evidence.
