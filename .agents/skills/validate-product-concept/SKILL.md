# Validate Product Concept

Build only enough solution fidelity to answer the highest-risk questions.

## Validate prerequisites

Require the selected problem, target users, evidence IDs, product direction, options-roadmap decision, and explicit learning questions. If missing, return a labeled draft, `FAIL`, or bounded `WAIVER`.

Do not invent participants, tasks performed, findings, quotations, threshold results, or approvals. Keep synthetic examples labeled and outside the research evidence set.

## Design validation

Read `references/concept-validation-framework.md`, then:

1. Critique one related and, when useful, one unrelated product for target user, problem, strong design choices, and improvement opportunities.
2. Identify the riskiest desirability, usability, feasibility, viability, and AI assumptions.
3. Define representative users, tasks, context, and accessibility needs.
4. Choose the lowest prototype fidelity that can answer the learning question.
5. Give each screen or step one clear goal; include only necessary content, inputs, actions, and navigation.
6. Define measurable success, failure, severity, and stop criteria before testing.
7. Collect consented evidence without coaching participants or inventing findings.
8. Assess technical feasibility and AI/data/model thresholds separately from user preference.
9. Record limitations, contradictions, segments, and learning sufficiency.
10. Recommend proceed, pivot, continue testing, hold, or stop.

Use `assets/concept-validation-template.md`.

## Apply the concept gate

Return `PASS` only with representative tasks/users, severity-based findings, adequate learning, feasibility evidence, AI thresholds where relevant, independent evaluation, and an accountable decision. A prototype alone is not proof.

## Output

Lead with the concept decision or next learning action. Include risks tested, prototype scope, participants/tasks, criteria, evidence, findings and severity, feasibility, AI evaluation, limitations, gate status, and one next action with owner.
