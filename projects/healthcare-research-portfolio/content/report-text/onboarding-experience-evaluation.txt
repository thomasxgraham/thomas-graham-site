Onboarding Experience
Evaluation Overview The onboarding process for our healthcare app is the critical first interaction
patients have when engaging with digital health services. Currently
, onboarding
requires multiple steps to register
, verify
, and connect medical records. Initial
feedback and observations
suggest
that the complexity of the experiencecoulddeter adoption and reduce satisfaction.
Three onboarding experiences will be evaluated:
1.
Current Process
(V3)
:
The existing multi-step flow in our app
/web portal
.
2.
Updated Iteration
(v4.1)
:
A
streamlined flow with fewer steps designed to
reduce friction
and time to engagement
.
3.
MyChart Native Experience
:
The baseline
default Epic-
provided onboarding
as a comparison benchmark
.
This study will establish baseline usability and satisfaction metrics to inform
design decisions and ensure our onboarding experience fosters trust, accessibility
,
and patient engagement
as new patients begin their health journey
.
Objectives
•
Measure
effectiveness, efficiency
, and satisfaction
across three onboarding
approaches.
•
Establish
baseline usability metrics
for future iterations.
•
Identify
pain points and qualitative insights
to guide refinement.
•
Compare the streamlined experience against both the current process and
MyChart’
s native onboarding.
Methodology Study Type:
•
Moderated usability test (
remote or in-person
)
•
Unmoderated usability test (
if Maze is available
)
Participants:
15–18 participants, balanced across age, tech comfort, and patient
status.
Incentives:
$120 for in-person observations and $60 for remote sessions Approach
:
Within-subjects (
each participant completes all three
experiences in
counterbalanced order
)
on both the mobile app and the web experiences.
Data Collection
•
Quantitative metrics:
•
Time to Completion
•
Single Ease Question (SEQ)
after each task
•
System Usability Scale (SUS)
after each flow
•
CSA
T (Customer Satisfaction)
at the end of the session
•
Qualitative data:
•
Observed behaviors, think
-aloud comments, post
-task interviews
, and
follow-ups.
Tasks Participants will be asked to simulate signing up for and onboarding into the
patient portal
. Sample tasks include:
1.
Current Flow
•
Download/open the app.
•
Register with an email and create a password.
•
Verify account through email/SMS.
•
Connect medical records.
2.
Current Flow with
Updated
Activation
•
Begin onboarding with a reduced-step process (fewer screens, simplified
verification).
•
Connect medical records.
3.
MyChart Native Flow
•
Access and onboard using the Epic MyChart
experience
.
•
Link records to their provider
.
Metrics Efficiency
•
Task Completion Rate (%)
•
Time on Task (seconds/minutes)
•
Error Rate (frequency and type of errors)
Perceived Ease
•
SEQ (1–7 scale after each task)
Usability
•
SUS (10-item standard survey after each flow)
Satisfaction
•
CSA
T (1–5 Likert scale at the end of session)
Qualitative Feedback
•
Open-ended debrief questions:
•
What was most confusing about the onboarding process?
•
At what point did you feel confident/doubtful in the process?
•
How does this compare to your expectations for signing up for a
healthcare service?
•
Out of the three
flows,
which one
did you prefer
and why
?

Deliverables
1.
Research findings deck with baseline metrics, task analysis, and key themes.
2.
Comparative usability benchmarks across all three flows.
3.
Recommendations for optimizing the onboarding experience prior to
