Findings Overview We conducted a site visit in the lobby of the Lake Mary Health Park facility to evaluate the
Request a Prayer
feature. Patients were invited to interact with the feature and share their
perceptions, expectations, and experiences.
Our goal was to understand:
•
Whether the feature feels authentic and supportive.
•
What kinds of support patients expect.
•
How usable the prayer request form is.
•
Preferences for channels of communication.
•
Overall impressions of the feature.
Key Findings Perception of Authenticity
•
We were unable to measure the sentiment towards any automated responses
,
as
patients
either did not want to include their mobile number or chose an option where
the mobile number was optionally entered
.
Expectations of Support
•
Patients
would appreciate
encouraging wordsor follow-up supportregardless of the
prayer option selected Effectiveness of Resources
•
When resources were provided, most found them
relevant and helpful
.
•
Opportunity: A library of varied resources (scripture, meditations, chaplain connections)
could broaden value
in follow-up interactions.
Preferred Channels of Support
•
Patients mentioned multiple preferred channels:
•
SMS
Text
was most frequently referenced.
Understanding of Options
•
Many patients showed
confusion between “Silent prayer
” and “
Prayer
.
”
•
There was also confusion about sending a prayer vs requesting a prayer
. Most patients
expect to say/send a prayer to someone else upon initial interaction.
•
Recommendation:
Arrange input to ensure prayer is top of mind vs asking what to do
with the prayer Experience with the Form
•
Difficulty was correlated with confusion over prayer options
and physical limitations
(ability to see/process UI)
.
•
Suggestion:
How might the form be more scannable during interactions?
Overall Impression
•
Nearly all participants rated their overall experience as
positive or very positive
and
were delighted about the feature.
•
Even patients with minor confusion or difficulty still felt
uplifted by the feature
.
•
This shows strong
emotional resonance
.
